Common questions

Asked before subscribing

What appears on my venue pin?

Your name, address, hours, phone number, open/closed status, and any category tags you provide. No markup, no ordering system — just the facts users need to decide whether to visit.

How do I update my venue details?

Through the listing desk on Telegram. Send a message with your updated hours, phone, or address and we apply the change to the live database.

Are there other pricing tiers?

Professional ($29/mo) and Premier ($49/mo) are planned but not yet available for purchase. Only Founding Professional ($9/mo) and City License ($199/mo) have active Stripe checkout today.

Do you take a cut of orders?

No. There is no ordering functionality. The radar shows information and lets users call you directly. Zero percent markup on anything.
