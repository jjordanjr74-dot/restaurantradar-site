Questions

Asked before people use it.

Is it free to search?

Yes. The map loads in any browser with no app download and no account.

Where does the venue data come from?

923 of the 932 venues come from OpenStreetMap, 8 from a public food-truck listing and 1 from the venue operator. Each record keeps its source.

Why do some pins say hours are unknown?

Only venues with published hours can show open or closed. Today that is 404 venues. The rest stay marked unknown rather than showing a guess.

Does the radar rate stops for safety or amenities?

Not yet. Safety and amenity ratings are not part of the map today, so none are shown. The map covers location, hours where published, and contact details where published.

Do you take a cut of orders?

No. There is no ordering on the radar. People see the venue and contact it directly.

How do I update my venue's details?

Message the listing desk on Telegram with your updated hours, phone or address.
